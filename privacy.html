Privacy Policy

Capra Falcon Inc — Last updated June 2026

Effective Date: June 8, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Capra Falcon Inc ("we", "our", "the Company") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with our website (caprafalcon.com) and our internal business applications, including the Capra Falcon Agent application which uses the Amazon Selling Partner API (SP-API).

2. Information We Collect

When you visit our website, we may collect the following:

  • Contact information you voluntarily submit through our contact form (name, email address, message)
  • Basic usage data collected automatically (page visits, browser type, referring URL) via standard web server logs

We do not use tracking cookies, advertising pixels, or third-party analytics on this website.

3. SP-API Application Data

The Capra Falcon Agent is a private internal application used exclusively by Capra Falcon Inc to manage our own Amazon seller account. Through the Amazon Selling Partner API, this application accesses:

  • Product listing content from our own Amazon seller account (titles, bullet points, descriptions, keywords, attributes)
  • Product catalog data including pricing, images, and sales rank
  • Advertising performance data from our own campaigns

We do not access, store, or process any customer personally identifiable information (PII), customer order data, payment information, or data from any Amazon seller account other than our own.

4. How We Use Your Information

Contact form submissions are used solely to respond to your inquiry. We do not sell, rent, or share your contact information with any third party.

SP-API data is used exclusively for internal listing optimization and business management purposes.

5. Data Storage and Security

  • Contact form data is stored securely and retained only as long as necessary to respond to your inquiry
  • SP-API credentials and accessed data are stored on private, password-protected business servers
  • No Amazon seller or customer data is stored in cloud databases or shared with external services
  • We use industry-standard security practices to protect all data in our possession

6. Third-Party Services

Our internal application uses the Anthropic Claude API for AI text generation. Only product content text is transmitted to Anthropic — no customer data, PII, or financial information. Anthropic's privacy policy is available at https://www.anthropic.com/privacy.

Google Workspace is used for business email. Google's privacy policy applies to all email communications.

7. Your Rights

If you have submitted information through our contact form and wish to have it deleted, please email us at support@caprafalcon.com and we will process your request promptly.

8. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Continued use of our website following any update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
